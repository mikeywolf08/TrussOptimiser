This is built by a bored 1st year engineer at the Uni of Camb, whom, after being told they have to analyse the bar forces for 3 or 4 different designs with three failure modes in compression, with Euler buckling data read off a terribly imprecise graph alongside increasingly erratic lengths/coordinates as the designs became more unhinged, whilst still having to optimise for weight. They gave up completely and decided to spend three hours coding this instead.

This optimises for weight given a set of bar lengths & loads for any truss you can think of as long as you have the bar lengths and their loads, which you may calculate by hand or via for example https://www.trussanalysis.com.

Put your data it in an Excel spreadsheet, in label | length | load columns, input into spreadsheet then into the script, adjust safety factor as needed, adjust types of bars in bar library as needed, press Run. Ctrl-V into Excel for your data!

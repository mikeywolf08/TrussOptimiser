This is built by a bored 1st year engineer
At the Uni of Camb
Whom, after being told they have to analyse the bar forces for 3 or 4 different designs
With three failure modes in buckling included
Read off a terribly imprecise graph
Alongside increasingly erratic lengths/coordinates as the designs became more unhinged
Whilst still having to optimise for weight
Gave up completely
And decided to spend three hours coding this instead

This optimises for weight given a set of bar lengths & loads for any truss you can think of
As long as you have the bar lengths and their loads
Which you may calculate by hand or via for example https://www.trussanalysis.com
Put it in an Excel spreadsheet, in label | length | load columns, and input into spreadsheet
Adjust safety factor as needed
Adjust types of bars in bar library as needed
Press enter
Ctrl-V into Excel for your data!
